You can cancel or reschedule free of charge with at least 24 hours notice, using the link in your confirmation email or by WhatsApp.

We allow 15 minutes of grace. After that the service may be shortened so the following clients are not affected, or rescheduled if it can no longer be completed. In both cases the booked service is charged.

If you do not arrive and do not let us know, we record it as a no-show. After the first we contact you to rebook; after the second we may ask for a deposit to confirm any future booking.

Prices published on this site and on our social media are "from" prices, meaning the starting point for each service.

The final price is confirmed at the salon after assessing your case: hair length and condition, nail length and condition, design complexity, product required and time needed. We always confirm the price before starting, and you decide whether to go ahead.

The outcome of any service depends on the prior condition of your hair, nails or skin: porosity, previous colour, bleaching, damage, length or the state of the nail plate.
