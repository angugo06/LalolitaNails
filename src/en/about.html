Our story

A neighbourhood salon, with an atelier soul.

Lalolita started from a simple idea: going to the salon shouldn't feel like an errand; it should be the best hour of your week.

By Eddu Vázquez · Since 2021

We started with one table and a lot of heart.

In 2021, in the middle of the San Rafael neighbourhood (the one with the theatres, the Jardín del Arte and the turn-of-the-century townhouses), Eddu Vázquez opened Lalolita with one conviction: technique can be taught, but caring about the details is something you bring with you.

What began as a nail studio grew alongside our clients. Today we also cut, style and colour hair, shape brows with thread, and do makeup for brides who trust us with the most important day of their lives. And in 2026 we took the biggest step yet: a second home in Polanco.

“Everyone who sits at our table leaves with something handmade, made for them.”

The road

From 2021 to today.

  • 2021: Lalolita is born

    We open on Guillermo Prieto 46 with a handful of polishes, one manicure table and a goal: do things properly.

  • 2022: hair joins the menu

    We add a styling station: cuts, blowouts and colour. The salon becomes a full plan for the day.

  • 2023: brow threading and makeup

    We bring in threading, the gentlest technique for brow design, and start doing makeup for events and brides.

  • 2024: three years and 16 thousand friends

    We celebrate our third anniversary with a community of more than 16,000 Instagram followers sharing every design.

  • 2026: we open in Polanco

    Our second location arrives at Lago Tanganica 61, in the Granada neighbourhood of Polanco. Same menu, same technique and the same care, now on that side of the city too.

  • Today: two homes, one standard

    San Rafael and Polanco share a team, standards and the same enthusiasm as day one. Pick whichever suits you: you're just as welcome at both.

What defines us

Three things we won't compromise on.

✦

Health first

Sterilised tools, quality products and technique that protects your nails, skin and hair. Pretty is no good if it isn't healthy.

✦

Never rushed

We work by appointment only so we can give your service the time it deserves. Nobody gets hurried or half-attended here.

✦

Made for you

Your style leads. Bring a photo, an idea, or just a “surprise me”, and we'll turn it into something that feels like yours.
