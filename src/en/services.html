FAQ

Before your first visit.

How do I book?

Online, from our booking page: you pick the location, the service, who looks after you and the time, with live availability and instant confirmation. The calendar is in Spanish, so if you would rather book in English just message us on WhatsApp at 55 6885 6070 (San Rafael) or 56 1515 6061 (Polanco) and we will set it up for you. Saturdays fill up fast, so book a few days ahead.

Is removal included?

Yes. If you're coming in for a new nail service, removing your previous gel or acrylic is on us.

How long will gel or acrylics last?

Gel looks flawless for two to three weeks. Acrylics need a fill every three to four weeks, depending on how fast your nails grow.

Do you take groups or events?

Of course: hen parties, quinceañeras, weddings and girls' days. Message us to block out time together and put a package together.

What if I need to cancel or change my appointment?

Your confirmation email has a link to reschedule or cancel yourself. You can also message us on WhatsApp. Please give us at least 24 hours notice, since your slot is held specially for you.

Do you speak English?

Yes, we're happy to look after you in English at both locations. Feel free to message us in English too.

Know what you want?

Tell us your idea, bring your Pinterest screenshot, or just let us spoil you. We'll take it from there.
