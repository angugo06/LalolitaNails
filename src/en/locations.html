Two neighbourhoods

From San Rafael to Polanco.

San Rafael is where we were born: historic theatres, neighbourhood cafés and turn-of-the-century townhouses. In 2026 we opened in Polanco, in the Granada neighbourhood, to be closer to the people who live and work on that side of town.

Both locations share the same menu, the same technique and the same enthusiasm. Pick whichever works for you.
