4. How to opt out of optional purposes

  • Email [contact email] with the subject "Optional purposes".
  • Reply BAJA to any WhatsApp message we send you.

Opting out does not affect your ability to receive the service or the quality of your care. We simply stop sending you commercial messages.

5. ARCO rights

Under Mexican law you have the right to Access your data, Rectify it when inaccurate, Cancel it when you believe it is no longer needed, and Object to its processing for specific purposes.

Send your request to [contact email] including:

  • Your name and a way to send you our reply.
  • A copy of official ID proving your identity, or your legal representative's along with the power of attorney.
  • A clear description of the data concerned and which right you are exercising.
  • Anything else that helps us locate your data.

We reply within 20 business days and, where applicable, act on it within the following 15 business days. The process is free of charge; only shipping or reproduction costs may apply if you request copies.

6. Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time through the same channel as ARCO requests. Note that in some cases we cannot act immediately if the law requires us to keep certain records, such as tax data.
