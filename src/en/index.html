The Lalolita spirit

A neighbourhood salon with an atelier soul.

“Beauty feels better when it looks entirely like you.”

Lalolita was born in 2021 in the San Rafael neighbourhood, founded by Eddu Vázquez as an alternative to the rushed salon: a warm space where design and the health of your nails matter equally, and where hair, brows and makeup get the same dedication.
