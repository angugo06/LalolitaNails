FAQ

About invoicing.

What is a CFDI?

The CFDI (Comprobante Fiscal Digital por Internet) is Mexico's official electronic invoice, validated by the SAT tax authority. You need one if you want to deduct the service as a business expense. If you're a tourist or don't file taxes in Mexico, you don't need it.

What is the Constancia de Situación Fiscal?

It's the document the SAT issues with your current fiscal details: RFC, name or company name, tax regime and postcode. You download it from the SAT portal and share it with us on WhatsApp so we can enter everything exactly as registered.

Why do you need my postcode?

Since CFDI version 4.0, the SAT checks that the name, RFC and fiscal postcode match its records exactly. If anything differs, the invoice is rejected.

How long does it take?

Usually the same day or the next business day. You'll get the PDF and XML at the email address you give us.

I lost my ticket — can I still get an invoice?

Message us on WhatsApp with the approximate date, the amount and the service. We'll check the records at that location and confirm.
